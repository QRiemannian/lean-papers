## 5 · Intended Lean statement
**Prose.** Every real quadratic number field K has discriminant d_K ≥ 5; and ℚ(√5) is a real quadratic field with d = 5. So 5 is the minimal real-quadratic discriminant.

**Proof route for L, and the development it needs (NEEDS-DEVELOPMENT, named).** For K real quadratic: r₂ = 0, so d_K > 0 (`sign_discr`) and `abs_discr_ge'` gives d_K ≥ 2⁴/2!² = 4. **Only d_K = 4 remains to exclude**, and that is the gap: show d_K is not a perfect square. One route: 1 is primitive in 𝓞_K (𝓞_K ∩ ℚ = ℤ), so 𝓞_K has a ℤ-basis {1, ω}; then d_K = b² − 4c for the minimal polynomial X² + bX + c of ω, and d_K = 4 forces b even and X² + bX + c = (X + b/2)² − 1, reducible — contradicting ω ∉ ℚ. The local lemma ("a quadratic field's discriminant is not a square", or "𝓞_K = ℤ[ω] with d_K = disc(minpoly ω)") is not at the pin. The attainment conjuncts are L:RZ-09 (b) plus finrank 2 (√5 ∉ ℚ, X² − 5 irreducible) and total reality (K ⊂ ℝ: every embedding into ℂ sends √5 to ±√5 ∈ ℝ).
