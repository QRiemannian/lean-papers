## 3 · Formal reading
- **The field.** ℚ(ζ₅), ζ₅ = e^{2πi/5}. Formally: any field K with `[IsCyclotomicExtension {5} ℚ K]` (a number field by `IsCyclotomicExtension.numberField`). The paper's concrete subfield of ℂ is one such K, and any two are ℚ-isomorphic (`IsCyclotomicExtension.algEquiv`), so quantifying over all of them says exactly what the print says about the field, no more — the invariant is isomorphism-invariant. A concrete alternative is `CyclotomicField 5 ℚ` (instances `IsCyclotomicExtension {n} K (CyclotomicField n K)` and `NumberField (CyclotomicField n K)` at Mathlib/NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/Basic.lean, the `CyclotomicField` namespace, lines ≈ 700–708); V₁ may prefer it.
- **Instance note.** The mathlib lemmas carry `[Fact (Nat.Prime p)]`; at the pin there is no global `Fact (Nat.Prime 5)` (only `Nat.fact_prime_two`, `Nat.fact_prime_three`, and a ℕ+ `fact_prime_five`). L supplies `⟨by norm_num⟩` locally; it is a provable fact, not a hypothesis of the statement. `NumberField` is a `Prop`-valued class (NumberField/Basic.lean:44), so the instance the lemma builds with `haveI` and the one in the signature agree by proof irrelevance.
- **Discriminant (convention named).** The absolute discriminant of the number field, mathlib's `NumberField.discr K : ℤ` (the discriminant of an integral basis of 𝓞 K), signed. For ℚ(ζ₅) the sign is + (r₂ = 2): `IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.discr_prime` gives (−1)^{(5−1)/2} · 5^{5−2} = (+1) · 125. The print's unsigned "5³ = 125" therefore matches the signed value exactly; the spec states the signed equality `= 125` (stating |d| = 125 would be weaker than what is true and than what is printed).
- No other hypotheses.
