- ζ is `riemannZeta`; p ranges over the primes (`Nat.Primes`); p^{−s} is `(p : ℂ) ^ (-s)` (`Complex.cpow`).
- **Domain supplied: 1 < Re s.** The served text still prints the product with no domain. The spec supplies the paper's evident domain — the half-plane where "the standard multiplicative decomposition" holds — and records the omission as a **wording note, not a CC**: the sentence names the standard identity, whose domain is standard, so it is not read as vacuous. (Without the domain the Lean statement would be false: mathlib's `tprod` of a non-multipliable family is 1, and e.g. ζ(0) = −1/2.)
- **Meaning of "=" with an infinite product:** the product converges (unconditionally, over the primes) to ζ(s). The faithful Lean form is `HasProd`, which asserts convergence and the value; the `tprod` form alone would be weaker (it holds vacuously-by-junk if the product failed to converge). The partial-product form over p < n (`riemannZeta_eulerProduct`) is an equivalent-strength alternative L may also state.
