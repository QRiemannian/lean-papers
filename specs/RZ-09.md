- **d_K = 5 (clause b).** `NumberField.discr K = 5` (mathlib's discriminant of the ring of integers, an integer).
- **h_K = 1 (clause c).** `NumberField.classNumber K = 1`.
- **"fundamental unit ε = φ" (clause d; convention named).** The fundamental unit of a real quadratic field: the unit ε > 1 (in the real embedding) with (𝓞 K)ˣ = {±εⁿ : n ∈ ℤ}. Stated as: φ_K is a unit (inverse φ − 1), its value is φ > 1, and every unit u is φ_Kⁿ or −φ_Kⁿ for some n ∈ ℤ.
