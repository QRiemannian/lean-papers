## 3 · Formal reading
- **Carrier.** The operators are elements of an arbitrary ring that is a ℂ-algebra: `{A : Type*} [Ring A] [Algebra ℂ A]`. The printed argument is ring-theoretic (parities of products); no representation, Hilbert space or spectrum enters.
- **Multiplicative ℤ₂ grading, (13.2).** A family `𝒜 : ZMod 2 → Submodule ℂ A` with `[GradedAlgebra 𝒜]` — an internal direct-sum decomposition with `𝒜 i * 𝒜 j ⊆ 𝒜 (i + j)` and `1 ∈ 𝒜 0`. **Convention:** the paper's multiplicative label ε ∈ {+1, −1} is the degree d ∈ ZMod 2 under ε = (−1)^d, so ε = +1 ↔ `𝒜 0`, ε = −1 ↔ `𝒜 1`, and (13.2) ε(AB) = ε(A)ε(B) is `SetLike.GradedMul.mul_mem` (degrees add).
- **Parity assignment, (13.1).** "ε(X) = ±1" is read as "X is homogeneous of that degree": hypotheses `H ∈ 𝒜 0`, `E ∈ 𝒜 1`, `S ∈ 𝒜 1`, `D ∈ 𝒜 1`. The labels "isotropic"/"anisotropic" carry no formal content.
- **Relation (3.2), as a HYPOTHESIS (Q7).** `E * S - S * E = (Complex.I * (ħ : ℂ)) • (H * D)`, with the commutator [X, Y] = XY − YX (mathlib's `Ring.lie_def` agrees) and iℏ the complex scalar `Complex.I * ħ`, `ħ : ℝ`. Only (3.2) is a hypothesis, because the printed argument uses (3.2) alone; (3.3)–(3.5) are not needed and not assumed.
- **ℏ ≠ 0.** ℏ is the (reduced Planck) constant; the print leaves its non-vanishing implicit. `hħ : ħ ≠ 0` is used only to pass from (iℏ)·ĤD̂ = 0 to ĤD̂ = 0.
- **"Incompatible", read formally.** Under the grading, the left side of (3.2) lies in `𝒜 0` (odd·odd) and the right side in `𝒜 1` (even·odd; scalars preserve each `𝒜 i`). Since `𝒜 0 ∩ 𝒜 1 = 0` (the decomposition), the relation can hold only with both sides zero: [Ê, Ŝ] = 0 and ĤD̂ = 0. This is exactly the printed parity mismatch, stated as its consequence: the relation is not homogeneous for the grading, so it is compatible with the grading only degenerately.

## 5 · Intended Lean statement
Prose: let A be a complex algebra with a ℤ/2-grading (a graded algebra decomposition indexed by ZMod 2). If Ĥ is homogeneous of even degree, Ê, Ŝ, D̂ are homogeneous of odd degree, ℏ ≠ 0, and relation (3.2) [Ê, Ŝ] = iℏ ĤD̂ holds, then both sides vanish: [Ê, Ŝ] = 0 and ĤD̂ = 0. (That is, the grading of (13.1)–(13.2) is compatible with (3.2) only when the relation is degenerate — the printed parity mismatch.)

```lean
/-- L:RZ-80 — RZ §13.2, (13.1)–(13.2), PDF p31; relation (3.2) from §3, PDF p09.
Under a multiplicative ℤ₂ grading with Ĥ even and Ê, Ŝ, D̂ odd, relation (3.2) holds only with
both sides zero. The relation is a hypothesis only (Q7): no structure or class carries the algebra. -/
theorem rz_80_z2_grading_incompatible
    {A : Type*} [Ring A] [Algebra ℂ A]
    (𝒜 : ZMod 2 → Submodule ℂ A) [GradedAlgebra 𝒜]
    (E S H D : A) (ħ : ℝ) (hħ : ħ ≠ 0)
    (hH : H ∈ 𝒜 0) (hE : E ∈ 𝒜 1) (hS : S ∈ 𝒜 1) (hD : D ∈ 𝒜 1)
    (h32 : E * S - S * E = (Complex.I * (ħ : ℂ)) • (H * D)) :
    E * S - S * E = 0 ∧ H * D = 0 := by sorry
```
Proof route for L (not part of the statement): `E * S`, `S * E` ∈ `𝒜 (1 + 1) = 𝒜 0` by `SetLike.mul_mem_graded`, so the left side ∈ `𝒜 0`; `H * D` ∈ `𝒜 (0 + 1) = 𝒜 1`, so the right side ∈ `𝒜 1` by `Submodule.smul_mem`; an element of `𝒜 0 ∩ 𝒜 1` is `decompose x 1 = x` and `= 0` (`decompose_of_mem_same` / `decompose_of_mem_ne`); then `smul_eq_zero` with `Complex.I * ħ ≠ 0`.

## 6 · What the row does not claim
- **Not** that no ℤ₂-graded realization of the relations by nonzero elements exists. Degenerate nonzero graded realizations of all four relations (3.2)–(3.5) do exist (for instance, in a product of two copies of the exterior algebra ℂ[ε]/(ε²) with ε odd, take Ê = (ε, 0), Ĥ = (1, 0), Ŝ = D̂ = (0, ε): every cyclically adjacent product vanishes and all four relations hold as 0 = 0). The row reads "incompatible" as the printed mismatch argument establishes it — the relation is not homogeneous, so it holds under the grading only with both sides zero — and no further.
- Nothing about (3.3)–(3.5), about the missing commutators of (13.3) (that is L:RZ-81), or about the Cartan decomposition that §13.4–§13.5 put in the grading's place (L:RZ-82).
- Not that the operator algebra exists, is consistent, or has any representation (Q7: RZ-18 is NOT A TARGET; the relation is a hypothesis only).
- Not the isotropic/anisotropic reading of the labels, the §13.1 heuristic ("two radial projections compose into a tangential one"), nor §13.5's spectral-separation reading of the Selberg trace formula.
