## Conventions fixed in this unit
- Types of SL(2, ℤ) elements use mathlib's discriminant predicates on the integer matrix (discr = tr² − 4·det). At det 1 these are the paper's |tr| conditions, except that the parabolic predicate excludes the scalars ±I (this is the issue in 41b; RZ-83 uses the non-scalar form).
- PSL(2, ℤ) is mathlib's SL(2, ℤ) ⧸ centre, with centre = {±1} proved in 43a (iv). PSL stabilizers are images of SL(2, ℤ) stabilizers.
- ∂ℍ = `OnePoint ℝ`, with GL(2, ℝ) acting by Möbius maps.
- λ (multiplier) is the eigenvalue of modulus > 1, and ℓ := 2 log |λ|. For t < −2 the paper's "log λ" is read with the modulus, and √(t² − 4) depends only on t².
- σ is 4 log φ as a number in 63a. "systole(X(1))" is the minimum translation length over hyperbolic elements in 63b.
- g_c = 1/(√5 · π) (abstract, p02) is "the geometry constant" in docstrings. It is written out in statements; no `def` is introduced.
- Gauss map: T(x) = Int.fract x⁻¹. Lean gives T(0) = 0, the standard convention; the print's formula is undefined at 0.
- ℒ_s is a `tsum` over n ≥ 1 of `((x + n : ℝ) : ℂ) ^ (-2 * s) * f (1/(x + n))`. It is 0 off summability, and no convergence region is claimed.
- Transfer-operator weight (53c only): |T′|^{−s}, not printed, named as the reading.
- Composition order in (8.6): reflection applied after STⁿ, M_n = R·(S·Tⁿ) with R = diag(−1, 1).
- Δ = −y²(∂²_x + ∂²_y), with the printed leading minus, for f : ℂ → ℂ evaluated at points of ℍ.
