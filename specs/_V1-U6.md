## 4 · The rulings asked for

1. **"Involves only the prime 5" — prime set = {5}, not ⊆ {5}.** "Involves … the prime 5" asserts that 5 occurs; the print's own gloss "(the Spiral eigenvalue squared)" names 5 as present; and only under "= {5}" is the [CV] sentence "each of the three singles out p = 5 on its own" true at the p = 2 edge (disc ℚ(ζ₂) = 1 has the empty prime set, ⊆ {5} vacuously). This is the plain reading of the words, so it is a reading, not a repair; RZ-71a leg 2 stands as specced.
2. **RZ-71b's `p ≠ 2` — a reading of the printed p*, not a repair; no CC-CANDIDATE.** "Its unique quadratic subfield is ℚ(√p*)" presupposes that a quadratic subfield exists, which fails only at p = 2 (ℚ(ζ₂) = ℚ); the printed definiens p* = (−1)^{(p−1)/2} p is an integer only for odd p (the standard convention of the cited Washington). At p = 2 the clause fails by presupposition, not by falsity, so it does not assert anything there. The hypothesis is also load-bearing in Lean: ℕ-division would make the exponent 0 and turn p* into 2, giving a false statement the print never made. (B) over all primes needs no such hypothesis (false at p = 2 by the packed existence, and 2 ≠ 5).
3. **RZ-72a clause 7 and RZ-71a's first condition — count ONCE.** "|Gal(ℚ(ζp)/ℚ)| = 4 ↔ p = 5" (Prop. 10.1, p26, "p − 1 = 4") and "[ℚ(ζp):ℚ] = 4 ↔ p = 5" (p27, "the only prime cyclotomic field of degree 4") are one fact printed at two loci, equal by `IsGalois.card_aut_eq_finrank`. Keep both statements, one per locus, for traceability; count one proof in the ledger (L proves one and derives the other in a line). Likewise RZ-72b (A) and RZ-71b (A) count once, as M already has it.
4. **RZ-72a's "cyclic quartic layer" as a ℤ/4ℤ subgroup of the Galois group — rightly so.** The print's own premise is "(ℤ/pℤ)* ≅ ℤ/(p − 1)ℤ **contains ℤ/4ℤ**", a subgroup statement; a subgroup H ≅ ℤ/4ℤ of Gal(K/ℚ) is the cyclic quartic top layer K/K^H, so "layer" is satisfied literally. The other reading (a cyclic quartic subfield, i.e. a ℤ/4ℤ quotient) is equivalent here because the group is cyclic (both hold iff 4 ∣ p − 1), so no statement is stronger or weaker than the print. No change.
