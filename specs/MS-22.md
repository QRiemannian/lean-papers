## 3 · Formal reading
- **"systole" (convention):** the minimal translation length over hyperbolic conjugacy classes of SL(2, ℤ) (the paper's own gloss after (3.5): "the standard restricted minimum, over closed geodesics arising from hyperbolic conjugacy classes"). That this minimum **equals** 4 log φ is (3.5)/(2.5), i.e. L:MS-06 = L:RZ-06b (EFFORT, out of scope). As L:RZ-15 does with vol(X(1)), this row takes the systole's value as a **hypothesis**: a real `systole` with `systole = 4 * Real.log φ`. The row's content is what (3.6) says about that value.
- log is `Real.log` (natural); φ is `Real.goldenRatio`.
- Claims: (i) ½ · systole = 2 log φ; (ii) "≈ 0.962" at its printed precision — three decimals, the half-unit interval |2 log φ − 0.962| < 0.0005 (same convention as L:RZ-16); (iii) "the systole is 4 log φ, never 2 log φ": systole ≠ 2 log φ (equivalently log φ ≠ 0, since φ > 1).
- Numerics (recomputed here, 40 digits): 2 log φ = 0.96242365011920689…; distance to 0.962 is 4.24 × 10⁻⁴, margin 7.6 × 10⁻⁵ — true but not loose. Interval plan for L (not a claim): 0.48075 < log φ < 0.48125, i.e. exp(0.48075) < φ < exp(0.48125); exp(0.48125) − φ ≈ 6.2 × 10⁻⁵ is the tight side, so φ is needed to ~6 significant figures (2.236067 < √5 < 2.236068 via `Real.lt_sqrt`/`Real.sqrt_lt'`) and exp(0.48125) to ~5 × 10⁻⁶ (`Real.exp_bound` with a degree-7 or 8 Taylor polynomial).

## 5 · Intended Lean statement
Prose: for any real `systole` equal to 4 log φ: half of it is 2 log φ; 2 log φ lies within 0.0005 of 0.962; and `systole` is not 2 log φ.

theorem ms_22_halfSystole (systole : ℝ) (h : systole = 4 * Real.log Real.goldenRatio) :
    systole / 2 = 2 * Real.log Real.goldenRatio ∧
