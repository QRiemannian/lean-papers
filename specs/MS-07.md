## 3 · Formal reading
- **Convention, named (not printed in MS):** the Selberg norm of a hyperbolic element γ of SL(2, ℝ) is N_Γ(γ) = λ², where λ > 1 is its eigenvalue of modulus greater than 1; for trace t > 2, λ = (t + √(t² − 4))/2. This is the standard normalisation under which log N_Γ(γ) is the translation length 2 log λ of (2.4), which is exactly the "matching" the sentence asserts. The spec defines N_Γ through the trace so that the row is about F², not about a free λ.
- F² is `!![2, 1; 1, 1] : Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) ℝ`; its trace is 3 (`Matrix.trace_fin_two_of`).
- φ is `Real.goldenRatio`; log is `Real.log` (natural).
- Claims: (i) N_Γ(F²) = φ⁴; (ii) "matching ℓ = 4 log φ through (2.4) with λ = φ²": the larger eigenvalue λ of F² is φ², log N_Γ(F²) = 2 log λ (the (2.4) length), and 2 log φ² = 4 log φ.
- "the systolic class" (that F² represents the smallest-trace hyperbolic class, hence the systole) is not taken here (L:RZ-03, L:MS-06).

## 5 · Intended Lean statement
Prose: with N_Γ of a hyperbolic class of trace t defined as ((t + √(t² − 4))/2)², and F² the matrix (2 1; 1 1): its trace is 3, the eigenvalue (3 + √5)/2 is φ², N_Γ(F²) = φ⁴, and log N_Γ(F²) = 2 log φ² = 4 log φ.

```lean
/-- The Selberg norm N_Γ of a hyperbolic element of trace `t` (> 2): λ², λ = (t + √(t² − 4))/2
the eigenvalue of modulus > 1 (Selberg's normalisation, log N_Γ = translation length). -/
noncomputable def selbergNormOfTrace (t : ℝ) : ℝ := ((t + Real.sqrt (t ^ 2 - 4)) / 2) ^ 2

/-- L:MS-07 — MS §2.2 (2.6), served p06 (sha256 b4841489…3af5). -/
theorem ms_07_selbergNorm_F2 :
    let F2 : Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) ℝ := !![2, 1; 1, 1]
    (F2.trace + Real.sqrt (F2.trace ^ 2 - 4)) / 2 = Real.goldenRatio ^ 2 ∧   -- λ = φ²
    selbergNormOfTrace F2.trace = Real.goldenRatio ^ 4 ∧            -- (2.6)
    Real.log (selbergNormOfTrace F2.trace) = 2 * Real.log (Real.goldenRatio ^ 2) ∧  -- = ℓ of (2.4)
    2 * Real.log (Real.goldenRatio ^ 2) = 4 * Real.log Real.goldenRatio := by sorry
```
