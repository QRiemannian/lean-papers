## 3 · Formal reading
- **The family and its index.** The (2, 3, r) triangle orbifolds are indexed by r; the hyperbolic members are the integers r ≥ 7 (the print names (2, 3, 7) as the infimum), and X(1) is the member r = ∞. The index set is taken as F = {r : ℕ∞ | 7 ≤ r} (`ENat`, ⊤ = ∞), with the printed convention 1/∞ = 0.
- **Area, as printed.** area(r) = 2π(1/6 − 1/r), a real function of r. The row states the arithmetic the paper prints; that this number is the hyperbolic area of the (2, 3, r) orbifold (Gauss–Bonnet) is not taken (EFFORT, see 6).
- **"strictly increasing in r":** StrictMonoOn area F.
- **"X(1) (at r = ∞, area π/3) is the family's supremum":** area(∞) = π/3, and π/3 is the least upper bound of the areas of the finite members r ≥ 7 (the supremum read as the limit the finite family approaches and X(1) attains).
- **"(2, 3, 7) … area π/21 — its infimum":** area(7) = π/21 and π/21 is the least element of the family's areas (attained, at the named member).
- **χ_orb, supplied (not printed in this sentence):** the orbifold Euler characteristic of the (2, 3, r) sphere orbifold, χ_orb(r) = 2 − (1 − 1/2) − (1 − 1/3) − (1 − 1/r) = 1/r − 1/6 (standard formula; at r = ∞ it gives −1/6, the value MS §4.3 prints for X(1), L:MS-42 = L:RZ-44a). The spec names it; V₁ decides whether the naming suffices.
- **"k = 1/(2|χ_orb|) runs from 21 down to 3 across the family":** k(7) = 21, k(∞) = 3, and k is strictly decreasing on F. (k(r) = 3r/(r − 6) for finite r; 21 at r = 7, 12 at r = 8, → 3.)
- **"the factor 3 … requires r = ∞ exactly":** for r ∈ F, k(r) = 3 ↔ r = ∞.
- **Not taken:** "Siegel's global minimum" (that (2, 3, 7) has the least area among all compact hyperbolic orbifolds / Fuchsian groups — a cited theorem, E4) and "the factor 3 that the framework reads" as a framework statement; the grade [D].

## 5 · Intended Lean statement

/-- 1/r on the (2,3,r) family, with the printed convention 1/∞ = 0. -/
noncomputable def ms02InvOrder (r : ℕ∞) : ℝ := if r = ⊤ then 0 else 1 / (r.toNat : ℝ)
/-- MS §2.1 p05: area of the (2,3,r) member, 2π(1/6 − 1/r) (as printed). -/

/-- MS §2.1 p05: k = 1/(2|χ_orb|). -/
noncomputable def ms02K (r : ℕ∞) : ℝ := 1 / (2 * |ms02ChiOrb r|)

/-- L:MS-02 — MS §2.1, served p05 (sha256 b4841489…3af5). -/
theorem ms_02_triangleFamily :
    let F : Set ℕ∞ := {r | 7 ≤ r}
    StrictMonoOn ms02Area F ∧
    ms02Area ⊤ = Real.pi / 3 ∧
    IsLUB (ms02Area '' {r | 7 ≤ r ∧ r ≠ ⊤}) (Real.pi / 3) ∧
    ms02Area 7 = Real.pi / 21 ∧
    IsLeast (ms02Area '' F) (Real.pi / 21) ∧
    ms02K 7 = 21 ∧ ms02K ⊤ = 3 ∧ StrictAntiOn ms02K F ∧
    (∀ r ∈ F, ms02K r = 3 ↔ r = ⊤) := by sorry
