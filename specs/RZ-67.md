## 3 · Formal reading
- **The field.** ℚ(ζ₅), ζ₅ = e^{2πi/5}. Formally: any field K with `[IsCyclotomicExtension {5} ℚ K]` (a number field by `IsCyclotomicExtension.numberField`). The paper's concrete subfield of ℂ is one such K, and any two are ℚ-isomorphic (`IsCyclotomicExtension.algEquiv`), so quantifying over all of them says exactly what the print says about the field, no more — the invariant is isomorphism-invariant. A concrete alternative is `CyclotomicField 5 ℚ` (instances `IsCyclotomicExtension {n} K (CyclotomicField n K)` and `NumberField (CyclotomicField n K)` at Mathlib/NumberTheory/Cyclotomic/Basic.lean, the `CyclotomicField` namespace, lines ≈ 700–708); V₁ may prefer it.
- **Instance note.** `IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.five_pid` itself takes no primality instance; any lemma L routes through that carries `[Fact (Nat.Prime p)]` (e.g. the discriminant lemmas) needs one, and at the pin there is no global `Fact (Nat.Prime 5)` (only `Nat.fact_prime_two`, `Nat.fact_prime_three`, and a ℕ+ `fact_prime_five`). L supplies `⟨by norm_num⟩` locally; it is a provable fact, not a hypothesis of the statement. `NumberField` is a `Prop`-valued class (NumberField/Basic.lean:44), so the instance the lemma builds with `haveI` and the one in the signature agree by proof irrelevance.
- **Class number (convention named).** h = the order of the ideal class group of the ring of integers, mathlib's `NumberField.classNumber K : ℕ` (= `Fintype.card (ClassGroup (𝓞 K))`). `NumberField.classNumber_eq_one_iff` turns h = 1 into "𝓞 K is a principal ideal ring", which is `IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.five_pid`.
- No other hypotheses.
