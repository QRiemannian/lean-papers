## 3 · Formal reading
- **The area function is not printed;** only its four values and the minimality are. The spec supplies the standard area of the (2, q, ∞) orbifold (the Hecke group's quotient, two copies of the triangle with angles π/2, π/q, 0): A(q) = 2π(1 − 1/2 − 1/q) = π − 2π/q (the ledger's "area formula π − 2π/q checked"). With it the four printed values are A(3), A(4), A(5), A(6). V₁ decides whether supplying A is acceptable (the printed list alone would make "the areas run …" a bare chain of four reals, true but detached from q).
- **Family index:** the Hecke family is the integers q ≥ 3 (q = 3 is X(1), as printed).
- **Claims:** (i) A(3) = π/3, A(4) = π/2, A(5) = 3π/5, A(6) = 2π/3 ("the areas run"); (ii) the printed chain π/3 < π/2 < 3π/5 < 2π/3; (iii) "X(1) (at q = 3) is area-minimal": A(3) is the least value of A over q ≥ 3.
- The listed four are read as the first four members (q = 3, 4, 5, 6), the natural reading of "run"; nothing is claimed about q = ∞.
