## 3 · Formal reading
- φ is `Real.goldenRatio` (= (1 + √5)/2, an `abbrev` at the pin); √5 is `Real.sqrt 5`; π is `Real.pi`; g_c is the geometry constant 1/(√5 · π) (shared definition, L:RZ-15).
- **Convention:** the Λ of (3.1) is the argument-less quaternary product, by the paper's own notation note — it is **not** `completedRiemannZeta` (that is Λ(s), L:RZ-46a). The Lean name must not reuse Λ; the statement is a plain real identity.
- Claim: φ · √5 · π · g_c = φ, with the middle step the substitution g_c = 1/(√5 · π). No hypotheses (√5 · π ≠ 0).

## 5 · Intended Lean statement
Prose: the product of the golden ratio, √5, π and the geometry constant equals the golden ratio; and the intermediate writing with g_c substituted equals it too.

```lean
/-- L:RZ-17 — RZ (3.1) p09: the argument-less quaternary product (NOT the completed zeta Λ(s)). -/
theorem rz_17_quaternary_product :
    Real.goldenRatio * Real.sqrt 5 * Real.pi * geometryConstant =
      Real.goldenRatio * Real.sqrt 5 * Real.pi * (1 / (Real.sqrt 5 * Real.pi)) ∧
    Real.goldenRatio * Real.sqrt 5 * Real.pi * (1 / (Real.sqrt 5 * Real.pi)) = Real.goldenRatio := by sorry
```
