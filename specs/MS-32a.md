## 3 · Formal reading
- **g_c as a function of the discriminant:** g(d) = 1/(π√d) for real d > 0 (`Real.pi`, `Real.sqrt`); at d = 5 it is the geometry constant 1/(√5 · π) (L:RZ-15's `geometryConstant`, up to the order of the product).
- **The ladder, integer form.** The discriminants of real quadratic fields are the fundamental discriminants D > 1 (the field identification is L:RZ-13, EFFORT, not taken here). With the local definition `ms13IsFundDisc` of L:MS-13 (shared; L places it once), the printed ladder start is: the fundamental discriminants D with 1 < D ≤ 21 are exactly 5, 8, 12, 13, 17, 21.
- **"attains its maximum … at d_K = 5" (integer form):** for every fundamental discriminant D > 1, g(D) ≤ g(5), with equality only at D = 5. (g is strictly decreasing in d > 0; 5 is the least ladder member, L:MS-13.)
- **"the next rung down is lower by exactly √(5/8) − 1":** the relative change from the rung d = 5 to the next rung d = 8 is g(8)/g(5) − 1 = √(5/8) − 1 (exact).
- **"= −20.943%":** read at the printed precision, three decimals of a percent: |100 · (√(5/8) − 1) − (−20.943)| < 0.0005. Recomputed: 100 · (√(5/8) − 1) = −20.94305849…; distance 5.85 × 10⁻⁵ (true, margin 4.4 × 10⁻⁴).
- **Not taken:** "is a field certificate" (a name), "form-independently", "[CV]"/"[D]", "safe to print without a prior frame choice", and the field form of the maximality (L:MS-32b).
