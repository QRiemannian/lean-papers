## Conventions fixed in this unit
- ℚ(ζn): any K with `[CharZero K] [IsCyclotomicExtension {n} ℚ K]` (`[NumberField K]` where `NumberField.discr` is used), all ℚ-isomorphic by `IsCyclotomicExtension.algEquiv` — the U1 convention, kept; `CyclotomicField p ℚ` where a set-builder must not quantify over types (RZ-72a clause 6, RZ-72c).
- Gal(K/ℚ) = `K ≃ₐ[ℚ] K`; "≅" = `Nonempty (_ ≃* _)`; ℤ/mℤ as a group = `Multiplicative (ZMod m)`; "contains ℤ/4ℤ" = a subgroup isomorphic to it.
- Discriminant: `NumberField.discr`, signed; "involves only the prime 5" = `natAbs.primeFactors = {5}`; "a power of p" = `∃ k, natAbs = p ^ k`.
- "Unique quadratic subfield is ℚ(√d)": `∃ x, x ^ 2 = d ∧ ∀ F : IntermediateField ℚ K, finrank ℚ F = 2 ↔ F = ℚ⟮x⟯`; p* = (−1)^{(p−1)/2} p for odd p.
- "p ≡ 1 (mod 4)": `p ≡ 1 [MOD 4]` (`Nat.ModEq`).
- Dedekind-zeta factorisation (RZ-72c): over *primitive* characters (the imprimitive trivial character mod p would miss the Euler factor at p), on 1 < re s.
