## 5 · Intended Lean statement
Prose: with q = e^{−2π}, for every natural k ≥ 1 the k-th term of the correction series of (4.3) equals the k-th term of the q-series of (5.1); hence the two series over k ≥ 1 are equal.

```lean
/-- L:RZ-32a — RZ (4.3) p11 ↔ (5.1) p14: the termwise q-rewriting of Ramanujan's correction series, q = e^{−2π}. -/
theorem rz_32a_correction_q_form :
    let q : ℝ := Real.exp (-(2 * Real.pi))
    (∀ k : ℕ, 1 ≤ k →
      1 / ((k : ℝ) ^ 3 * (Real.exp (2 * Real.pi * k) - 1)) = q ^ k / ((k : ℝ) ^ 3 * (1 - q ^ k))) ∧
```
