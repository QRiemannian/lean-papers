3. **Formal reading**
   - T, ℒ_s are `RZ.gaussMap`, `RZ.mayer` of `L:RZ-53a`.
   - **(C1)** For v ∈ [0, 1), the continued-fraction expansion of v is the one mathlib computes, `GenContFract.of v`, from the integer–fraction stream `GenContFract.IntFractPair.stream v`. "T generates it" is read as: the fractional parts of the stream are the T-orbit of v, and the partial denominators are ⌊1/Tⁿ(v)⌋ — `stream v 0 = some ⟨0, v⟩`, and whenever `stream v (n + 1) = some p`, `p.b = ⌊(T^[n] v)⁻¹⌋` and `p.fr = T^[n + 1] v`. (The stream stops exactly when some Tⁿ(v) = 0, i.e. v rational.)
   - **(C2) Convention named here** (the print says "its transfer operator" without printing the definition of a transfer operator): the transfer (Ruelle–Perron–Frobenius) operator of T at parameter s is ℒ_s f(x) = Σ_{y : T y = x} |T′(y)|^{−s} f(y). The claim that (8.3) is this operator is read as three facts for x ∈ (0, 1): (a) the T-preimages of x in (0, 1) are exactly the points 1/(x + n), n ≥ 1, each once; (b) T is differentiable at 1/(x + n) with T′(1/(x + n)) = −(x + n)²; (c) hence ℒ_s f(x) equals the sum over preimages with weight |T′(y)|^{−s} = ((x + n)²)^{−s} = (x + n)^{−2s}. **The endpoint x = 0 is left out:** its preimages 1/n are jump points of T, where T has only a one-sided derivative; the print does not say which derivative it means there.
   - (C2)(c) is an identity of `tsum`s under the reindexing n ↦ 1/(x + n), so it holds as an equation in Lean whether or not the series converges (both sides are reindexings of one family).
