## 3 · Formal reading
- φ is `Real.goldenRatio` = (1 + √5)/2; φ⁻² = (φ²)⁻¹; 1/φ = φ⁻¹; √5 = `Real.sqrt 5`; log = `Real.log`.
- Claims, all real identities with no hypotheses (φ > 0):
  (i) φ² − φ⁻² = φ + 1/φ;
  (ii) φ + 1/φ = √5;
  (iii) φ − 1/φ = 1, and φ − 1/φ ≠ √5 ("is 1, not √5");
  (iv) "the norm N = φ⁴ rather than the multiplier φ² (which doubles the numerator to 4 log φ)": log(φ⁴) = 4 log φ, and this is twice the multiplier's log(φ²) — log(φ⁴) = 2 · log(φ²).
- The "norm"/"multiplier" words fix which real number enters the logarithm (φ⁴ vs φ²); the row checks the arithmetic of the two choices, not that φ⁴ is the Selberg norm of F² (that dictionary is L:RZ-05/L:RZ-07 territory) and not (9.7) itself.
